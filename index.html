About Zelqo

Zelqo explores how much information you can put in a pair of glasses while keeping the scene easy to see. Studio lets you try different layouts and compare where a short cue or caption fits best.

What does a preset save?

It saves the scenario, enabled widgets, position, color, text size, background and contrast. Export the JSON file to share the same settings or use them in another browser.

Where does the data come from?

Studio uses scripted examples. Directions, captions and timers update as you move through the demo frames.

Can I try the glasses?

The frame and software platform are a concept. You can try the display in your browser using Studio.
